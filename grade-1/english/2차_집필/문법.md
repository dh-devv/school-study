# 영어 문법

## 1. 계속적 용법의 관계대명사 `, which`

앞의 내용에 대해 추가로 설명할 때 **`, which`**를 쓴다. 아래 예시에서는 앞의 사건이 가져온 결과를 설명한다.

> **주의:** 쉼표 뒤의 계속적 용법에는 `that`을 쓰지 않는다.

### 예시

Ben received a good review of his book. It encouraged him to keep writing.

→ Ben received a good review of his book, **which** encouraged him to keep writing.

The use of plastic bags was prohibited. It reduced waste and pollution.

→ The use of plastic bags was prohibited, **which** reduced waste and pollution.

## 2. 동사 + 목적어 + to부정사

일부 동사는 **동사 + 목적어 + to부정사** 형태로 쓴다.

### 예시

- Liah **asked me to visit** the restaurant with her.
- The teacher **required students to read** the book twice.
- A bear appeared and **caused the children to run** away.

## 3. 안내문에서 문법 확인하기

> Dear visitors, please be advised that our museum will close 30 minutes early this week. Pieces for a new exhibit are being delivered and will require us work longer hours to get them ready. We encourage you visiting our website for further updates on the new exhibit. Thank you for your cooperation.

| 원래 표현 | 올바른 표현 | 구조 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| require us **work** | require us **to work** | require + 목적어 + to부정사 |
| encourage you **visiting** | encourage you **to visit** | encourage + 목적어 + to부정사 |
